Istio Capabilities

Traffic Management

Weighted canary routing (80/20 split) and path-based routing via VirtualService. Automatic retries on transient failures.

Mutual TLS

Strict mTLS enforcement across all services. Istio-managed X.509 certificates ensure encrypted, authenticated communication.

Fault Injection

Inject 5s delays (50%) and HTTP 503 aborts (10%) into service-b without touching application code. Test resilience on demand.

Circuit Breaker

Outlier detection ejects unhealthy pods after consecutive 5xx errors. Connection pool limits prevent overload cascading.
